Inspiration

Our Goal

We want to push the bounds of what robots can do. We will create a robot able to traverse any terain at any angle using suction technology and efficent mechanical engineering.

    Mission Statement: Everything can be built upon

    Plans For The Future

    Giecko Inspired Climbing

    • Uses van der walls forces to cling to any surface
    • Allows for limitless movement and functionality

    Swarms

    • Create a way for spider to collaborate and acomplish tasks
    • Designate different spiders for different roles
    • Distribute the load of a task by using multiple systems

    1. Mechanical Layout

    Body Plate

    • Hexagon, 100mm flat-to-flat
    • 3mm carbon-fiber or 1.5mm aluminum
    • 6 servo mounts at 60° intervals

    Leg Architecture

    • Femur: 25mm, lift, sub-micro servo
    • Tibia: 20mm, spring-steel or silicone foot
    • Linkages: 0.8mm piano wire, M1 ball-links, M1×4mm screws

    3. Frame & Mounting

    • 3D prints in 2mm PLA/PETG with servo pockets
    • Carbon-fiber leg plates with M1 pivots
    • Silicone-padded spring feet for grip

    4. Electrical Assembly

    • Battery → Switch → Buck → 5V bus
    • TESP8266 → Servos
    • Compact perfboard layout, clean wiring
    • Camera: ESP32-CAM (Later iteration)

    5. Software Outline

    • Control MC in assembly for first iterations
    • Arduino IDE
    • Adafruit_PWMServoDriver, ZeroTimer
    • Tripod gait, 20ms update loop

    6. CAD & Fabrication

    Design

    • Fusion360/Onshape → DXF for cutting
    • STL exports for 3D prints

    Build

    • Laser cut & print parts
    • Solder & assemble electronics
